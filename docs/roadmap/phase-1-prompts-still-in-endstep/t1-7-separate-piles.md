When an opponent casts Fact or Fiction, separate the cards into two piles (Pile 1 / Pile 2) by dragging or clicking, instead of picking cards from a fan.

## What exists now

Endstep asks it as a plain card choice (the cards picked make one pile), which the board showed as a fan to pick cards from.

## What it involves

- Show it as two piles, like scry; the cards in pile 1 are sent.
- Recognize it by its message (a pile) or by the card asking.

## Done when

- The piles are made on the board, seeing both.
