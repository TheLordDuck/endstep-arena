## What exists now

Between games of a best-of-three, the sideboarding prompt (`CHOOSE_CARDS` with `contextType: "sideboard"`) steps aside to Endstep's UI.

## What it involves

- A full-screen view with the deck and the sideboard side by side, as in Arena; drag or click to move cards between them.
- Read in Endstep's bundle what the prompt carries and what answer it expects before designing.
- Group by mana value or type so a 60-card deck reads well.

## Done when

- Sideboarding is done entirely on the Arena board, and the answer sent is the same one Endstep sends.
