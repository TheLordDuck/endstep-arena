Arena has no step tracks across the middle: above the main button a short row of icons (main phase, combat with sword, shield and star for attackers, blockers and damage, second main, end) marks the current step with a flame, blue on the opponent's turn and orange on yours. Seen in MTG Arena gameplay videos (LegenVD, 2026-10-13).

## What exists now

Two full step tracks run along the life totals, and their gold dots set the stops.

## What it involves

- The compact indicator as in Arena; the stops move to it or to the settings.
- Needs approval because it removes the current tracks.

## Done when

- The current step shows above the main button, as in Arena, and stops can still be set.

## Why rejected

The user rejected it on 2026-10-14: the step tracks stay. The tracker records no other reason.
