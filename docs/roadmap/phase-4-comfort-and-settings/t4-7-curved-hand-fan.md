A visual change to something that exists. In Arena the hand is an arched fan (each card turned a little and lower toward the ends) with each card's mana cost readable at the top; hovering raises a card straight and larger, and on the mulligan screen the seven cards show in a large tilted fan (I3hPJQyUBV4 15:05, oKhLGseypS4 8:05, jHPVD5KdP1g 3:25).

## What exists now

A straight row peeking from the bottom, and the same on the mulligan screen.

## What it involves

- Curve the hand with a rotation and offset by position (the same idea as `layoutFans`) without breaking dragging to cast or reordering.

## Done when

- The hand and the mulligan's hand show in an arched fan, and dragging and reordering still work.
