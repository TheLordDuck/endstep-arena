Pass priority until combat, end of turn or your next turn from a menu, as Endstep allows.

## What exists now

Only *End turn* and *Hold priority* exist. Endstep has pass until combat, end of turn, your next turn, etc. (`onPassUntil…` in its client).

## What it involves

- A menu by the main button with those options, sending the same action Endstep sends.

## Done when

- You can pass until a point in the turn from the board.
