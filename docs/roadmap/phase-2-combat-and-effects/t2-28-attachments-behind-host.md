Asked for by the user, with a screenshot of Arena.

## What existed before

Attached cards stood behind their permanent, each showing a strip above it.

## What was done

- Auras and Equipment sit whole behind the permanent they're attached to, each peeking out from its left edge and a little above (below, on the opponent's side), the furthest one first.
- With more than one, a click on any of them opens them in a fan ("Attached") to pick one: the click does what it does on the table (target it, pick it, activate it, e.g. Equip). With only one, the click goes straight to it.

## Done when

- In harness `auras`, Pacifism, Rancor and Bonesplitter peek out from Grizzly Bears, and a click opens the fan.
- In harness `auras-target`, a Disenchant targets Rancor picked in the fan.
- Checked in the harness, not yet in a live match.
