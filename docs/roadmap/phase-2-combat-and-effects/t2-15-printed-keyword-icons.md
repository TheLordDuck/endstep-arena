Arena puts small icons along the bottom of each permanent for its keyword abilities (flying, deathtouch, lifelink, trample, vigilance, first strike, haste, reach, menace, indestructible, hexproof…), so the table reads without hovering. Seen in MTG Arena gameplay videos (LegenVD, 2026-10-13).

## What exists now

Only keywords an effect added (green) or took away (red) show on the permanent.

## What it involves

- Read the printed keywords from the card's Oracle text and show them as discreet icons.
- Hovering still opens the glossary; gained and lost keywords keep their colors.

## Done when

- A Serra Angel on the table shows flying and vigilance icons.
