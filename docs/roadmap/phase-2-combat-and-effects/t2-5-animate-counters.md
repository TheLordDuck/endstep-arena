When +1/+1 or -1/-1 counters change, or power and toughness do, the number jumps and the change shows.

## What exists now

Counters and power/toughness change without animation.

## What it involves

- Make the number jump and show `+1/+1` or `-2/-2` briefly on a change.
- Detect it by comparing the previous and new state, as the board already does for life.

## Done when

- Every change of counters or power/toughness is visible.
